Open issues, sent to the voice-router thread:
- Tests 7 and 10: a misheard or extra word picks the wrong tree on the first sentence, and later
  answers can't change topic. 6ab65fb adds topic words and `rescue()`. Replayed on these clips, it keeps
  choking/CO first, but "choking ... can't breathe" then opens the allergy card. It isn't on the board yet.
- wfa-choking: "can't breathe" fills only `conscious=yes`, not `breathing`, and there is no life
  rule for a choking person who isn't breathing.

Fixed on the tether side during the run:
- The bridge used the raw fit-gap reject, which threw away long clear sentences. It now uses the
  calibrated reject (`decode()`, `reject_calib.json`), and an empty decode asks again.
- Screens: the test number on every screen, "say the answers" on forms, "Hold BACK 1 sec: next test"
  on cards, and a fresh start screen whenever the board (re)connects.
- Button hold vs tap was the main confusion. TALK and BACK both need a hold, and a tap does something else.
